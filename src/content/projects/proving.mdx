opener: "Proving lets you test an AI agent on thousands of made-up customers before it ever meets a real one. The customers have goals, details they only give when asked, and patience that runs out. The agent's tools are recorded or faked, so nothing real is touched. At the end you get one answer, ship or hold, with the numbers behind it. It is tried here on two agents from this portfolio: Warden, which approves changes to live software, and Parley, which books property viewings by phone in English and Gulf Arabic."
